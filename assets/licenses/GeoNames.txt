Barkits includes a derived Philippine locality index from GeoNames.
Source: https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/PH.zip
Attribution: GeoNames, https://www.geonames.org/
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The index contains Philippine administrative seats and other populated places
with a listed population of at least 15,000. Barkits selected fields, joined
province names where available, and sorted the result. Coordinates represent
locality centers. Coverage and population counts depend on the source.
